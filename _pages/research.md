Ali Farhani's research interests sit at the intersection of **blockchain**, **FinTech**, and **digital finance**.

Areas of focus include:

- DeFi institutionalization, tokenization, and regulation
- Blockchain-enabled transparency and digital asset systems
- Digital Product Passports for sustainable lithium battery and electric vehicle supply chains
- Blockchain research related to cryptocurrency products and digital finance
