**Ali Farhani** is a **Product Manager | FinTech | Web3 & Blockchain** professional with a background in Industrial Engineering.

His work spans product management, product ownership, cryptocurrency products, digital finance, decentralized finance, tokenization, and blockchain-based systems. He currently works as a Product Owner at IraniCard, where his experience includes cryptocurrency and OTC exchange products.

Alongside industry work, Ali contributes as a Persian Translator at the Ethereum Foundation. His research interests include DeFi institutionalization, digital asset tokenization, blockchain-enabled transparency systems, and Digital Product Passports for sustainable lithium battery and electric vehicle supply chains.
